Philosophy

The philosophy

A xenolith is a fragment of rock caught inside a different rock and carried up, unchanged, from deep below. We photograph in the same spirit: one piece of light, kept exactly as it was.

Every frame is shot on location and edited with restraint, so what you see is what stood in front of the lens.

The work is not about spectacle. It is about presence. Standing in a place long enough for the light to settle, then keeping that exact moment without forcing it into something louder.
